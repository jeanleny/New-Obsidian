The channel operator is considered as the owner of the channel.
They have a certain power on the channel.
There can be several level of channel moderation
- halfops(half operator)
- protected(protected user/operator)
- founder(channel founder)
And any other the server wishes to define.


The channel operator may only use these command
- [[KICK]]
- [[MODE]]
- [[INVITE]]
- [[TOPIC]]

The channel moderator are identified by the channel member prefix (**'@'** for standard operator, **'%'** for halfops, protected, founder).
